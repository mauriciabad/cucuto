Cucuto plays a different character’s sound and vibration for each WhatsApp contact, like a cuckoo clock announcing who’s there. A cat for your sister, a trumpet for your boss. Phone in your pocket, you already know who wrote.

<b>Read this first: Cucuto needs root and LSPosed.</b> It is a tool for phones that already have:
• <b>Android 17.</b> It does not run on Android 16 or older.
• <b>Root</b>, for example with Magisk, KernelSU or APatch. Cucuto does not root your phone for you.
• <b>LSPosed</b> that loads modern modules (libxposed API 102). You enable Cucuto there with the System Framework scope and reboot.
• <b>WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business</b>, with its own notification tone and vibration left on.

Without root and LSPosed it can’t change WhatsApp’s sounds, but the sample chat lets you hear and feel any character in the app. Cucuto is an early version: expect rough edges, and keep your root manager’s safe mode in mind in case the phone ever fails to boot.

<b>How it works</b>
• <b>Import a sound pack.</b> Open a .soundpack.zip file, download one from a pack repository, or make your own in the app. A pack is a cast of characters: creatures with short clips and their own vibration, or songs to ring with.
• <b>Give a contact a character.</b> Pick a chat, tap a character to hear it, tap again to assign. Then pick their ringtone, or skip it.
• <b>Hear who it is.</b> When they message you, the notification plays their character’s voice and vibration.

<b>More than a new tone</b>
• Phone ringtones: a song from a pack, the phone’s default, or theirs left as it is. Cucuto can always give the old ringtone back.
• Ringtone rules in plain words: a song can ring live by day and as a radio cut at night. Read the pack’s rules, or write your own for one contact.
• Clips with good timing: a greeting for the first message of the day, a reply to a burst of messages, rare easter eggs, night and holiday clips. No sensors, no tracking, no battery cost.
• Big packs fold into sections, search finds a character by name, and Surprise me picks one at random.
• Add people from your contacts before they write: their voice plays from their second message.

<b>Built to stay out of the way</b>
• One sound, no duplicates: no second notification and no permanent icon in the status bar.
• Your settings still rule: muted chats, Do Not Disturb and silent mode work exactly as before, because Android still decides whether a message alerts.
• Safe fallback: if the module is off or anything fails, the normal tone plays. You never miss a message because of Cucuto.
• Groups too: one character for the group, or a voice for each person in it.
• A vibration per character, so you can tell who wrote with the phone on vibrate. Make your own in the editor, or tap out the rhythm.
• Nothing runs in the background: no service, no persistent notification, no battery exemption.

<b>What it touches</b>
• The module runs inside Android’s system process, where notifications are handled, and only looks at notifications from WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business. It never hooks the WhatsApp app and never reads what your messages say.
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. The app goes online only when you browse or download packs from a pack repository. Location is asked once, approximate, and only if you want day and night clips. Contacts are read only for phone ringtones and when you add a chat from your contacts.
• Cucuto never checks for updates by itself. Update it where you installed it from, then reboot: the module loads the new version at boot.

The app ships empty: no third-party content comes with it, and you are responsible for the packs you import. Free CC0 demo packs, the install guide and the pack format are at https://cucuto.mauri.app
