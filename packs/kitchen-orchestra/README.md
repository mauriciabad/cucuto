# Kitchen orchestra

A Cucuto demo pack: the kitchen as a percussion section.

| Character | Clips | Haptic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bong the pot | bong, clang | custom: heavy thud envelope |
| Ting the glass | ting, clink | custom: two ticks |
| Swish the whisk | shake, swish | auto |
| Tootle the kettle | whistle, bubbling | custom: three low ticks |

## Provenance

Everything here was made for this repository and is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0-1.0](LICENSE):

- Sounds are synthesized by [`packs/generate.main.kts`](../generate.main.kts) with ffmpeg (decaying sine partials and
  filtered noise). No recordings or samples.
- Sprites and the cover are hand-written SVG.
