# Chiptune critters

A Cucuto demo pack: four tiny 8-bit friends.

| Character | Clips | Haptic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bleep the frog | ribbit, croak, hop; ringtone "Pond song" with day and night variants | custom: two clicks |
| Tweet the bird | chirp, trill, whistle | custom: three ticks |
| Mew the cat | mew, purr, mrrp | auto |
| Bzz the bee | buzz, zip | custom: soft envelope |

## Provenance

Everything here was made for this repository and is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0-1.0](LICENSE):

- Sounds are synthesized by [`packs/generate.main.kts`](../generate.main.kts) with ffmpeg (`aevalsrc` square, saw and
  sine waves). No recordings or samples.
- Sprites and the cover are hand-written SVG.
